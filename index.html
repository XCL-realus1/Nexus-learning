Level 02 · Evaluate the Evidence

The data is conflicting.

Management says the Monday meeting drives accountability. Your data says something different. Choose the most defensible course of action.

Situation Report

Following your root cause analysis, you present findings to the Operations Director. He insists the two-hour Monday meeting is "essential for alignment." However, an anonymous staff survey shows 78% consider it the least productive part of their week. Three departments that piloted asynchronous updates last quarter showed a 22% improvement in on-time delivery. Leadership wants a recommendation before Friday's board meeting.

A
Keep the Monday meeting. The Operations Director has more context than the survey and changing it could damage leadership trust.
B
Recommend a structured pilot: replace the Monday meeting with async updates for two departments over four weeks, then compare delivery data before scaling.
C
Cancel all meetings immediately. The survey and delivery data are clear — action is needed now, not more deliberation.
D
Commission a full independent audit before making any recommendation. No change should happen without complete information.
